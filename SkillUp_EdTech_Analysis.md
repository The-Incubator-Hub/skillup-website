## Slide 1 {#slide-1}
**Title:** SkillUp EdTech – ONE YEAR PROGRAM PLAN

**Key Message:** A comprehensive workstream report covering free training, webinars, secondary schools, and an online academy for a year.

---

## Slide 2 {#slide-2}
**Executive Overview**
- Four‑pillar model to bridge Nigeria’s national skills gap.
- Goal: Transition from social‑impact focus to a high‑scale commercial engine.
- Annual revenue target: **₦70 M**.
- Four workstreams, 4‑month cycles, agile rollover.
- Milestones: 200 free subscribers (base), **10 k paid subscribers** by year‑end.

---

## Slide 3 {#slide-3}
**Problem Statement**
- **600k+** graduates per year lack job‑ready digital skills.
- **72 %** of Nigerian employers cite tech‑skill shortages.
- Less than **5 %** of graduates possess basic coding or cybersecurity competence.
- SkillUp EdTech aims to close the gap from classroom to career‑ready.

---

## Slide 4 {#slide-4}
**Who We Serve** – Three integrated learner segments:
1. **AI Secondary** – Students 11‑18 (30 schools YoY). Free training embedded in curriculum.
2. **Free Training** – Recent graduates & job seekers 18‑30 (₦25 k commitment model). Target **1 200+** trained experts/year.
3. **Online Academy** – Working adults 22+ (flexible subscription). Goal **10 k** paid subscribers by year‑end.

---

## Slide 5 {#slide-5}
**Product Offering**
- Advanced tech courses (Data, Cyber, AI, Design, Development) with ₦25 k commitment fee.
- Monthly tech advocacy & industry innovation sessions.
- Specialized physical classes for accountants, administrators, and children/undergrads.
- Strategic workstreams: Free Training Webinars, AI for Professionals, Summer Training, and in‑house personal training.
