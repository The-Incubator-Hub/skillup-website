## Executive Summary
- SkillUp EdTech is a one‑year, four‑pillar program aimed at closing Nigeria’s digital‑skills gap.
- **Revenue target:** ₦70 M annual.
- **Subscriber targets:** 200 free pilots → 10 k paid subscribers by year‑end.
- **Commitment fee:** ₦25 000 per learner (covers onboarding & admin).
- **Pricing tiers:** Crash Course ₦2 500, Full Course ₦10 000, Monthly Subscription (≈₦2 000/month).
- **Projected gross revenue from LMS Academy:** ≈ ₦47.5 M (70 % crash courses, 30 % full courses).

---

## Mission & Vision
**Mission:** Empower Nigerian learners – from secondary‑school students to working adults – with industry‑ready digital skills through accessible blended training and a scalable online academy.

**Vision:** Become the continent’s leading provider of competency‑based EdTech that bridges the skills gap, fuels economic growth, and creates sustainable revenue streams.

---

## Market Analysis
**Problem:**
- 600 k+ graduates per year lack job‑ready digital skills.
- 72 % of Nigerian employers report tech‑skill shortages.
- < 5 % of graduates possess basic coding or cybersecurity competence.

**Opportunity:** A large, underserved learner base across three segments (AI Secondary, Free Training, Online Academy) creates a multi‑billion‑naira market for upskilling services.

---

## Product Description
**Core Offering (Four‑pillar model):**
1. Free Training (blended hybrid) – entry point, filters low‑intent users with a ₦25 k commitment fee.
2. Monthly Webinars – lead generation, brand awareness, on‑demand vault.
3. AI Secondary School Program – B2B partnership with 10‑30 schools, embedded curriculum.
4. Online Academy – subscription‑based, self‑paced LMS with certification.

**Curriculum Highlights (Slide 8):**
- Tracks: Cybersecurity, Data Analysis, Product Design, Software Development, AI for Work.
- Emphasis on AI tools, UI/UX, full‑stack development, forensic/ethical hacking, business intelligence.

**Delivery Mechanics (Slides 9‑10):**
- Automated enrollment via webhooks; weekly drip content; live project work and certification.
- Public website acts as marketing funnel; LMS portal handles secure delivery, progress tracking, recurring subscriptions.

---

## User Personas
1. **AI Secondary – Students (11‑18)**
   - Target: 30 schools, free training embedded in standard curricula.
2. **Free Training – Recent graduates & job seekers (18‑30)**
   - Commitment fee ₦25 k, aim for 1 200+ trained experts/year.
3. **Online Academy – Working adults (22+)**
   - Flexible subscription model, target 10 k paid subscribers by year‑end.

---

## Business Model & Pricing
**Pricing Structure:**
- Acceptance/Commitment Fee: ₦25 000 per learner.
- Tier A – Crash Course: ₦2 500 (single micro‑purchase, lifetime access).
- Tier B – Full Course: ₦10 000 (premium track + certification, lifetime).
- Tier C – Monthly Subscription: All‑access pass, auto‑revoked on lapse (≈₦2 000/month).

**Revenue Streams:**
- Commitment fees (baseline revenue).
- Subscription revenue from Online Academy.
- Paid crash‑course and full‑course sales.

---

## Go‑to‑Market Plan
**B2C Campaigns:**
- LinkedIn & Meta ads targeting accountants, administrators, parents in Lagos/Ogun.
- Short‑form video (Reels/TikTok) showcasing success stories.
- Funnel: free webinar → free training → paid academy.

**B2B School Program:**
- Target 10‑30 secondary schools in Lagos/Ogun.
- Pitch decks, case studies, partnership portal for school administrators.
- Corp‑member facilitators (1 NYSC corp per 5 schools).

---

## Product Roadmap
- **Q1 (M1‑3):** Pilot free‑training in 10 schools; finalize curricula for 15 crash courses; build web‑to‑LMS webhook.
- **Q2 (M4‑6):** Launch paid Online Academy (subscription tier); scale webinars, add 2‑3 new certifications/month; reach 2 k paid subscribers.
- **Q3 (M7‑9):** Expand school partnership to 30 schools; introduce holiday bootcamps; target 5 k paid subscribers.
- **Q4 (M10‑12):** Hit 10 k paid subscribers; achieve ₦70 M revenue; optimize cloud for 50 k concurrent learners.

---

## Technical Architecture
**Two‑engine design (Slide 6):**
- **Public Website:** marketing, funnel, e‑commerce, pricing visibility.
- **LMS Portal:** automated learner provisioning, content hosting, performance tracking, recurring subscription handling, certification issuance.

**Key Integrations:**
- Payment gateways (Paystack / Flutterwave).
- Webhooks to trigger LMS account creation and drip content.
- Cloud‑native stack capable of 50 k+ concurrent learners by Year 3.

---

## Implementation Checklist (Slide 24)
- Finalize curricula for 15+ crash courses & core tracks.
- Script facilitator presentations, set video recording standards.
- Post‑production schedule for steady releases.
- Connect web forms → LMS via webhooks/API.
- Deploy recurring billing (Paystack/Flutterwave).
- Configure LMS grading engine for auto‑issued certificates.

---

## Risks & Mitigations
- **Content Production Delays** – lock curriculum early, modular video templates, outsource post‑production.
- **School Partnership Slippage** – signed MOUs, phased rollout, dedicated corp‑member liaison.
- **Payment Gateway Failures** – integrate both Paystack and Flutterwave, fallback manual invoicing.
- **Scalability Bottlenecks** – cloud‑native architecture, auto‑scaling containers, load‑testing before Q3.
- **User Acquisition Cost** – performance‑based ad spend, leverage free webinars as lead magnets.
